Worth is on the army-cost scale: the fit is scaled so that, across all pieces, worth equals cost on average. Worth (army) comes from a logistic regression of each game's result on the difference in each piece type between the two starting armies (budgets were varied so worth can be measured on its own). Worth (on board) regresses results on the material on the board in quiet mid-game positions, for a fresh piece (shield intact, ability unused). It is more precise, but it only sees a piece while it's on the board: a Lance's promotion or a Ram's one capture don't show up there.
